Syllabus

We give a classical introduciton to basic graph theory following one of the standard texts on the subjects, West's "An introduction to Graph Theory." We introduce basic concepts with more of a focus on proof than calculation, and touch on some of the easier classical theorems. We will try to cover the first 6 chapters of the text book. Here is an approximate schedule.
